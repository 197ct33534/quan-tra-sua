A Ritual of Joy

Our signature Brown Sugar Bliss is a masterpiece of textures and temperatures. We slow-cook our Grade-A tapioca pearls in house-made Okinawan brown sugar syrup for 4 hours until they achieve a perfect "QQ" chewiness. Layered with fresh organic whole milk and a whisper of premium Assam black tea, each sip is a swirling dance of caramel sweetness and refreshing creaminess.
